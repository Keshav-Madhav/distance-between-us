HOW IT PLAYS

The whole world and nothing to win. Leave the ground — off a jump, a cliff, a rock's top, or Space at speed — and you fly; touch down anywhere and drive on. Same keys on the ground and in the air. Nothing hurts.

Beams mark trails of five crystals: on the ground, on the summits, up in the sky.

Keep climbing and you leave the air: point the nose up and hold Shift. Climb out past 1,500 km (Shift cruises up there) and you are back among the worlds.

HOW IT PLAYS

Take off and hunt in the open sky. Six waves: darts in V-flights, lancers that fire down their nose, interceptors that lock you from behind.

Lead your shots on the gold ring. Kills score more the faster you fly.
